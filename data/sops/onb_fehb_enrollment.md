# New employee health benefits enrollment (FEHB)
source: 5 CFR 890.301, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-890/subpart-C/section-890.301
---
An employee who becomes eligible may elect to enroll or not to enroll within 60 days after becoming eligible.
An enrollment takes effect on the first day of the first pay period that begins after the date the employing office receives an appropriate request to enroll and that follows a pay period during any part of which the employee is in pay status.
When an employing office determines that an employee was unable, for cause beyond his or her control, to enroll within the time limits, the employee may enroll within 60 days after the employing office advises the employee of its determination.
