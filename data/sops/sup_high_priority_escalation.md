# High-priority ticket escalation (authored)
source: AUTHORED for this project as a companion to sup_ticket_triage_auto_reply; queue and priority mix from the 16,335-ticket dataset used in agent-replay-console; not a public or Oracle document
---
A ticket predicted as high priority is never sent automatically.
The support agent confirms the priority and the queue within 1 hour of arrival.
If the ticket reports a service outage, the support agent notifies the on-call engineer and opens an incident.
The on-call engineer acknowledges the incident and posts a status update to the customer.
If the issue is a billing or payment error, the support agent forwards the ticket to the billing team with the customer's account details.
The support agent sends the customer an acknowledgement with the next update time.
When the issue is resolved, the owning team documents the fix in the knowledge base and closes the ticket.
