# Employment eligibility verification (Form I-9)
source: 8 CFR 274a.2(b) (DHS/USCIS), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-274a/subpart-A/section-274a.2
---
An employer that hires an individual for employment must ensure that the individual properly completes section 1, Employee Information and Verification, on the Form I-9 at the time of hire and signs the attestation.
If an individual is unable to complete the Form I-9 or needs it translated, someone may assist him or her.
Within three business days of the hire, the employer must physically examine the documentation presented by the individual establishing identity and employment authorization and ensure that the documents presented appear to be genuine and to relate to the individual.
Within three business days of the hire, the employer must complete section 2, Employer Review and Verification, on the Form I-9 and sign the attestation.
The employer must retain the Form I-9 for three years after the date of the hire or one year after the date the individual's employment is terminated, whichever is later.
If an individual's employment authorization expires, the employer must reverify on the Form I-9 not later than the date work authorization expires; otherwise, the individual may no longer be employed.
The employer shall be provided with at least three business days notice prior to an inspection of Forms I-9 by officers of an authorized agency of the United States.
