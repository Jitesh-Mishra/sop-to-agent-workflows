# Federal incident coordination with CISA
source: CISA Federal Government Cybersecurity Incident and Vulnerability Response Playbooks, https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-08/Federal_Government_Cybersecurity_Incident_and_Vulnerability_Response_Playbooks_508C.pdf
---
The agency notifies CISA within 1 hour of incident determination as directed by OMB M-20-04.
Where applicable, the agency notifies its appropriate Congressional Committees, its Office of Inspector General, and OMB Office of the Federal Chief Information Officer.
Within one hour of receiving the initial report, CISA provides the agency with a tracking number for the incident and a risk rating based on the National Cyber Incident Scoring System.
The affected agency shares relevant log data, cyber threat indicators with associated context, and recommended defensive measures with CISA and sharing partners throughout the containment phase.
CISA, in coordination with the intelligence community and law enforcement, shares related cyber intelligence to involved organizations.
The agency reports incidents to federal law enforcement as appropriate.
CISA or the FBI determines if the incident warrants Cyber Unified Coordination Group escalation, and, if so, recommends establishment of a C-UCG.
The agency provides CISA post-incident updates and a final incident report as required.
Agencies must proactively provide completed incident response checklists and a completed incident report to close the ticket.
CISA will evaluate these materials and determine that the incident is adequately addressed and close the ticket, or request that the agency complete additional response actions prior to closing the ticket.
