# Invoice receipt, review and payment
source: FAR 32.905 and 5 CFR 1315.4 (Prompt Payment), https://www.acquisition.gov/far/32.905 and https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-1315
---
Payment will be based on receipt of a proper invoice and satisfactory contract performance.
The designated payment office will annotate each invoice and receiving report with the actual date it receives the invoice.
Each invoice will be reviewed by the designated agency office as soon as practicable after receipt to determine whether the invoice is a proper invoice.
A proper invoice must include the contract number, a description, quantity, unit price and extended price of supplies delivered or services performed, the person to notify in the event of a defective invoice, and the Taxpayer Identification Number.
When an invoice is determined to be improper, the agency shall return the invoice to the vendor as soon as practicable after receipt, but no later than 7 days after receipt.
The agency will identify all defects that prevent payment and specify all reasons why the invoice is not proper and why it is being returned.
All invoice payments must be supported by a receiving report or other Government documentation authorizing payment.
When payments are made after the due date, interest will be paid automatically in accordance with the procedures provided in this part.
