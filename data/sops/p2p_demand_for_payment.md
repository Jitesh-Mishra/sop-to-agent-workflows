# Contract debts: demand for payment
source: FAR 32.604, https://www.acquisition.gov/far/32.604
---
Issue the demand for payment as soon as the contracting officer has determined that an actual debt is due the Government and the amount.
Issue the demand for payment even if the contractor has agreed to repay the debt.
Issue the demand for payment as a part of the final decision, if a final decision is required.
The demand for payment shall include a description of the debt, including the debt amount, a distribution of the principal amount by lines of accounting, and the basis for and amount of any accrued interest or penalty.
If the debt affects multiple contracts and the lines of accounting are not readily available, the contracting officer shall issue the demand without the distribution and provide the distribution by the date identified in the demand for payment.
The demand shall advise the contractor to contact the contracting officer if the contractor believes the debt is invalid or the amount is incorrect.
The contracting officer shall furnish a copy of the demand for payment to the contractor by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by any other method that provides evidence of receipt.
The contracting officer shall forward a copy of the demand to the payment office.
The contractor may submit a request for installment payments or deferment of collection if immediate payment is not practicable or if the amount is disputed.
