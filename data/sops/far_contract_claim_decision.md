# Contract disputes: claims and the contracting officer's decision
source: FAR 33.206, 33.207, 33.211 (48 CFR), https://www.acquisition.gov/far/33.211
---
Contractor claims shall be submitted, in writing, to the contracting officer for a decision within 6 years after accrual of a claim, unless the contracting parties agreed to a shorter time period.
Contractors shall provide the certification when submitting any claim exceeding $100,000.
When a claim by or against a contractor cannot be satisfied or settled by mutual agreement and a decision on the claim is necessary, the contracting officer shall review the facts pertinent to the claim.
Secure assistance from legal and other advisors.
Coordinate with the contract administration officer or contracting office, as appropriate.
Prepare a written decision that shall include a description of the claim or dispute, a reference to the pertinent contract terms, a statement of the factual areas of agreement and disagreement, and a statement of the contracting officer's decision, with supporting rationale.
For claims over $100,000, if a decision will not be issued within 60 days, the contracting officer shall notify the contractor, within that period, of the time within which a decision will be issued.
The contracting officer shall furnish a copy of the decision to the contractor by certified mail, return receipt requested, or by any other method that provides evidence of receipt.
If you decide to appeal, you must, within 90 days from the date you receive this decision, furnish written notice to the agency board of contract appeals and provide a copy to the Contracting Officer.
Instead of appealing to the agency board of contract appeals, you may bring an action directly in the United States Court of Federal Claims within 12 months of the date you receive this decision.
