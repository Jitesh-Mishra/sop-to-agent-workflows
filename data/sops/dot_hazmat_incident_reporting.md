# Hazardous materials incident notification and reporting
source: 49 CFR 171.15 and 171.16 (PHMSA), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-171/subpart-B/section-171.15
---
A telephone report is required whenever any of the listed incidents occurs during the course of transportation in commerce (including loading, unloading, and temporary storage).
As soon as practical but no later than 12 hours after the occurrence of any such incident, each person in physical possession of the hazardous material must provide notice by telephone to the National Response Center (NRC) on 800-424-8802.
Each person in physical possession of a hazardous material at the time of a reportable incident, an unintentional release of a hazardous material, or the discovery of an undeclared hazardous material must submit a Hazardous Materials Incident Report on DOT Form F 5800.1 within 30 days of discovery of the incident.
