# Medicare Part D coverage determination
source: 42 CFR 423.568, 423.570 and 423.572 (CMS), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-423/subpart-M
---
When a party makes a request for a drug benefit, the Part D plan sponsor must notify the enrollee and the prescriber of its determination as expeditiously as the enrollee's health condition requires, but no later than 72 hours after receipt of the request.
For an expedited request, the plan sponsor must provide an expedited determination if applying the standard timeframe may seriously jeopardize the life or health of the enrollee.
A Part D plan sponsor that approves a request for expedited determination must make its determination and notify the enrollee and the prescriber of its decision no later than 24 hours after receiving the request.
If the Part D plan sponsor makes a completely favorable decision, it must give the enrollee written notice of the determination.
If the plan sponsor denies the request, the notice of denial must state the specific reasons and inform the enrollee of the right to a redetermination.
If the Part D plan sponsor fails to notify the enrollee of its determination in the appropriate timeframe, the failure constitutes an adverse coverage determination, and the plan sponsor must forward the enrollee's request to the IRE within 24 hours of the expiration of the adjudication timeframe.
