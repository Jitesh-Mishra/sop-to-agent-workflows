# Medicare Advantage standard organization determination (prior authorization)
source: 42 CFR 422.566(d) and 422.568, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-422/subpart-M
---
For a service or item subject to the prior authorization rules, the MA organization must notify the enrollee of its determination no later than 7 calendar days after receiving the request for the standard organization determination.
The MA organization may extend the timeframe by up to 14 calendar days if the enrollee requests the extension or the extension is justified and in the enrollee's interest.
If the MA organization expects to issue a partially or fully adverse medical necessity decision, the organization determination must be reviewed by a physician or other appropriate health care professional with expertise in the field of medicine appropriate for the services at issue before the MA organization issues the decision.
The MA organization must give the enrollee and the physician or provider involved a written notice if it decides to deny a service or an item, in whole or in part.
The notice of any denial must use approved notice language, state the specific reasons for the denial, and inform the enrollee of his or her right to a reconsideration.
If the MA organization fails to provide timely notice of an organization determination, this failure itself constitutes an adverse organization determination and may be appealed.
