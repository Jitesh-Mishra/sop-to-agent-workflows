# Prior authorization for DMEPOS items
source: 42 CFR 414.234(d) and (e) (CMS), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-B/part-414/subpart-D/section-414.234
---
A prior authorization request must include all relevant documentation necessary to show that the item meets applicable Medicare coverage, coding, and payment rules, including the written order/prescription, relevant information from the beneficiary's medical record, and relevant supplier produced documentation.
The request must be submitted before the item is furnished to the beneficiary and before the claim is submitted for processing.
After receipt of a prior authorization request, CMS or its contractor reviews the prior authorization request for compliance with applicable Medicare coverage, coding, and payment rules.
If applicable Medicare coverage, coding, and payment rules are met, CMS or its contractor issues a provisional affirmation to the requester.
If applicable rules are not met, CMS or its contractor issues a non-affirmation decision to the requester.
If the requester receives a non-affirmation decision, the requester may resubmit a prior authorization request before the item is furnished and before the claim is submitted.
A prior authorization request for an expedited review must include documentation that shows that processing the request using a standard timeline could seriously jeopardize the life or health of the beneficiary.
