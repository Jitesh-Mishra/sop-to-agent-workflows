# Requests for major disaster declarations
source: 44 CFR 206.36 (FEMA), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-206/subpart-B/section-206.36
---
When a catastrophe occurs in a State, the Governor of a State, or the Acting Governor in his/her absence, may request a major disaster declaration.
The basis for the request shall be a finding that the situation is of such severity and magnitude that effective response is beyond the capabilities of the State and affected local governments, and that Federal assistance is necessary.
The complete request shall include confirmation that the Governor has taken appropriate action under State law and directed the execution of the State emergency plan.
The complete request shall include an estimate of the amount and severity of damages and losses, information on State and local resources committed, preliminary estimates of the supplementary Federal assistance needed, and the Governor's certification of compliance with cost sharing requirements.
The request must be submitted within 30 days of the occurrence of the incident in order to be considered.
The 30-day period may be extended by the Assistant Administrator for the Disaster Assistance Directorate, provided that a written request for an extension is submitted by the Governor during this 30-day period.
The Governor should submit the request to the President through the appropriate Regional Administrator to ensure prompt acknowledgment and processing.
For catastrophes of unusual severity and magnitude when field damage assessments are not necessary, the Governor may send an abbreviated written request through the Regional Administrator.
In the event the FEMA Regional Office is severely impacted by the catastrophe, the request may be addressed to the Administrator of FEMA.
Upon receipt of the request, FEMA shall expedite the processing of reports and recommendations to the President.
The Assistant Administrator for the Disaster Assistance Directorate shall assure that documentation of the declaration is later assembled to comply fully with these regulations.
