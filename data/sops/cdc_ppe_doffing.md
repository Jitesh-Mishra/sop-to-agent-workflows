# How to safely remove personal protective equipment (PPE), example 1
source: CDC, How to Safely Remove PPE (CS250672-E), https://www.cdc.gov/hai/pdfs/ppe/ppe-sequence.pdf
---
Remove all PPE before exiting the patient room except a respirator, if worn.
Gloves: using a gloved hand, grasp the palm area of the other gloved hand and peel off first glove.
Slide fingers of ungloved hand under remaining glove at wrist and peel off second glove over first glove, then discard gloves in a waste container.
Goggles or face shield: remove goggles or face shield from the back by lifting head band or ear pieces.
If the item is reusable, place in designated receptacle for reprocessing; otherwise, discard in a waste container.
Gown: unfasten gown ties, taking care that sleeves don't contact your body when reaching for ties.
Pull gown away from neck and shoulders, touching inside of gown only, turn gown inside out, and fold or roll into a bundle and discard in a waste container.
Remove the respirator after leaving the patient room and closing the door.
Mask or respirator: grasp bottom ties or elastics of the mask/respirator, then the ones at the top, and remove without touching the front, then discard in a waste container.
If your hands get contaminated during removal of any item, immediately wash your hands or use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer.
Wash hands or use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer immediately after removing all PPE.
